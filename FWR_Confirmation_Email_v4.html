Policies
Check-In Time — 4:00 PM
Registered guest must be over 21 years of age.
A valid credit card and photo ID are required upon check-in.
Early check-in is subject to availability may incur an additional charge.
Check-out Time — 10:00 AM
Late check out is subject to availability may incur an additional charge.
Non-Smoking
All villas at FantasyWorld Resort are non-smoking. Smoking is only permitted on the screened-in patio of the villa, or in designated smoking areas throughout the public spaces of the property.
Pets
We are a pet friendly resort and allow up to 2 pets no larger than 65 lbs each. Cats or dogs only. Some aggressive breed restrictions apply. Pets are accepted on prior notification only in select villas and require additional fees and a waiver. View the full pet policy here.
Security Deposit
A $100 authorization hold will be placed on your card for incidental room charges and will be released by us at checkout and can take as much as 7-10 business days to be released to you by your bank.
Resort Fees
Resort Fee of $29.99 + 13.5% tax per night includes Wifi, Parking, plus full access to Pools, Waterslides, Lazy River, Splash Pad, 18-Hole Mini Golf Course, Sports Park with Rock Wall & Batting Cages, Park Shuttle Service, and much, much more.
Housekeeping
Our villas are not hotel rooms- they are entire townhomes which require more time and effort to clean and maintain. To keep our rates affordable for families on a budget we only provide housekeeping services once at the beginning of the stay. Each villa is equipped with a washer/dryer and we provide laundry detergent free of charge, so we kindly ask that families wash and dry their own towels. Towels changes are available for a small fee. Additional housekeeping services are also available for an extra fee.
Room Requests
We will do our absolute best to meet requests for room locations which are handled as occupancy and maintenance allow, but we cannot guarantee that requests will be honored.
Cancellation Policy
Unless qualified as a non-refundable rate, for bookings made directly through the resort, the deposit is refundable unless the reservation is cancelled or modified within 48 hours prior to check-in. For reservations made through a third party, the deposit and cancellation policies of the third party shall apply to the reservation, and all changes must be made through them. Deposits for reservations made within the cancelation period will be considered non-refundable. A change in the length of your reservation or a change in dates may result in a rate change.
Miscellaneous
We will match rates from third parties which can be documented and verified as legitimate and authorized for sale by the resort. All discounts must be stated at the time of booking. The resort does not combine offers or discounts. All villas have a maximum occupancy of 6 persons. Amenities passes for additional day guests are subject to availability and an additional charge.
